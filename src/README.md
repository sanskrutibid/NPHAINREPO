
### Apply form without your own server
If `server/apply-server.js` is not running, the form automatically sends through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) instead:
every field and the resume are emailed to hr@phianinfotec.com. One-time step: submit one test application yourself,
then open the "Activate FormSubmit" email that arrives at hr@phianinfotec.com and click the link. After that every
application arrives in that inbox. (Resume up to 5 MB, PDF/DOC/DOCX.)
