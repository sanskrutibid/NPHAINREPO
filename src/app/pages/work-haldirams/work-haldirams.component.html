About the client

Haldiram's is a much-loved food and snacks brand, known to families across the country.

The brief

Food brands win on familiarity and appetite appeal. The work was to keep a well-loved name distinctive, and turn that affection into content, PR and digital reach that brings people back.

What we did

Positioning

Clarified what makes the brand distinct in a crowded food category.

Branding

Kept the look, tone and presentation consistent across packaging-led and digital touchpoints.

PR

Built media presence and goodwill around launches, festivals and brand moments.

Digital Marketing

Ran content, social and search activity designed to make people hungry and keep them coming back.

Hospitality and F&B approach

Food and F&B marketing is won on taste, trust and timing. We plan content, PR and digital activity around festivals, launches and everyday cravings, so the brand stays in front of people at the moment they choose.
