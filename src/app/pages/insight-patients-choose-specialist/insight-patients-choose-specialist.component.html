How patients choose a specialist now that AI answers come first

Patients now ask AI assistants and Google for recommendations before they ask a friend. Clinics need to be visible, and believable, in both.

DG
Debojyoti GhoshFounder, Phian. 5 min read

A parent with a sick child at night, or an adult with knee pain that will not go away, often starts with a search. Increasingly, that search is a question typed into an AI assistant or answered at the top of Google.

The answer engines look for evidence

AI assistants and Google's AI Overviews summarise what they can find and trust. A clinic with a complete Google Business Profile, consistent details across the web, clear service pages and genuine reviews is far easier for them to recommend.

Trust still decides the booking

Visibility gets a clinic onto the shortlist. Trust decides the booking. Patients look for a doctor's qualifications, a calm and informative tone, and signs that other patients had a good experience.

Speed matters more than ever

When a patient calls or sends a WhatsApp message, the first clinic to reply well often wins. Slow responses undo all the work that went into being found.

A simple checklist for clinics

  • Complete and update your Google Business Profile every week.
  • Create a clear page for each service and each doctor.
  • Ask every satisfied patient for a review, and reply to every review.
  • Publish short, doctor-led explainers on common questions.
  • Reply to calls and WhatsApp messages within minutes, not hours.
