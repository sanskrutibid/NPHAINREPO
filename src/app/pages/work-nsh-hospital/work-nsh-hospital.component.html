About the client

NSH Critical Care Hospital and Diagnostic Centre is a hospital built around critical care and diagnostics, under the promise of Excellence in Care.

The brief

People choose a hospital in moments of worry. NSH needed a brand that signals readiness and skill before a family ever walks in, and a digital presence that is easy to find and easy to trust.

What we did

Positioning

Defined what NSH stands for in critical care and diagnostics, and why a family should trust it over the other hospitals they consider.

Branding

Shaped one identity, voice and look for every touchpoint, from print to social.

PR

Built the hospital and its doctors' reputation through media and profile work, within medical ethics.

Digital Marketing

Ran campaigns, local search and content that make the hospital easy to find when people need it.

Healthcare approach

Healthcare marketing works differently from other sectors. Claims must be accurate, the tone must be calm, and every channel must respect patient privacy and medical ethics. This mandate was planned with those rules first.
