About the client

Mukunda Group was founded in 1971 by Mukundbhai Ved and is now led by the next generation of the Ved family. Its best-known brand, Swetal Logistics, is an established name in logistics and warehousing. The group also works in transport, steel and real estate.

Its real estate arm, Mukunda Infraventures, develops residential and industrial plots in Nagpur.

The brief

A group that runs several businesses and several land projects needs one identity that holds them together. Mukunda Group came to Phian to redesign its group logo and brand palette, and to present its plotted projects as one portfolio.

The work covered brand, search and PR, so that the group and each of its projects could be recognised and found under one name.

What we did

Brand

Redesigned the group logo and brand palette so that the group's businesses read as one family.

Architecture

Organised the group and its projects into one brand system. Each project keeps its own identity while carrying the group's name and look.

Content

Created the plotted project portfolio, presenting each project's layout, location and details in one consistent format.

Search

Strengthened the search presence of the group and its projects, so buyers looking for plots in Nagpur can find them.
