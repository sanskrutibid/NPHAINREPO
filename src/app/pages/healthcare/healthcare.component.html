Questions we often hear.

Do you follow medical advertising guidelines?

Yes. We avoid claims, before-and-after misuse and anything that conflicts with medical council guidelines. Doctors approve every clinical statement.

Can the doctor appear on camera without much time?

Yes. We plan batch shoots so a doctor can record a month of content in a couple of hours.

Do you manage Google reviews?

Yes. We set up review requests and draft responses for your approval, without sharing any patient details.

Can you build our clinic website?

Yes, including doctor profiles, services, booking and WhatsApp integration.
