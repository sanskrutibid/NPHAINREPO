About the client

Swetal Logistics is the logistics and warehousing brand of the Mukunda Group. Its services include covered and open warehousing, road and rail transport, material handling and a private railway siding in Nagpur. It works with companies in steel, pharma, automotive, FMCG, agro and paints.

Nagpur sits at the geographic centre of India, which gives logistics operators there quick access to major cities and ports.

The brief

For its logistics park, Swetal needed to reach occupiers and developers across the country, not only in Nagpur. Phian rebuilt the brochure around the questions occupiers ask and ran national demand generation.

What we did

Brand

Rebuilt the positioning and brochure around the questions occupiers ask.

Content

Created films, decks and posts for logistics decision makers.

Media

Ran national demand generation targeted at occupiers and developers.

Web

Built landing pages with enquiry tracking.
