How to get your business named by ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews

Search is changing from ten blue links to one written answer. Here is how local and regional businesses can make sure they are part of it.

DG
Debojyoti GhoshFounder, Phian. 7 min read

When someone asks an AI assistant for the best interior designer in Nagpur or a reliable logistics park near the ring road, the assistant writes an answer. Your business is either in that answer or it is not.

What AI search is looking for

AI systems favour businesses they can understand clearly and verify easily. That means a well-structured website, consistent business details across listings, specific information about what you do and where, and signs of real reputation such as reviews and media mentions.

Write for questions, not keywords

People ask AI assistants full questions. Pages that answer those questions directly, in plain language, are easier to quote. A frequently asked questions section on each service page is a simple place to start.

Make your details consistent everywhere

Your name, address, phone number and services should match across your website, Google Business Profile, directories and social profiles. Inconsistency makes it harder for AI systems to trust any version.

Where to begin

  • Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google what they say about your business and your category today.
  • Fix titles, descriptions and structured data on your website.
  • Create one clear page per service and per location.
  • Build reviews and credible mentions steadily over time.

This work, often called GEO or generative engine optimisation, builds on good SEO rather than replacing it. The businesses that start now will be the ones AI assistants learn to recommend.
