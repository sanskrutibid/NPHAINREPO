About the client

Baidyanath is an Ayurveda brand that stands on the promise of Asli Ayurved, genuine Ayurveda.

The brief

A heritage Ayurveda name has to feel trusted and current at the same time. The work was to keep the Asli Ayurved promise clear while speaking to buyers who now search, compare and decide online.

What we did

Positioning

Kept the Asli Ayurved promise at the centre of how the brand is described and recognised.

Branding

Aligned the identity and voice so a heritage brand looks and sounds consistent everywhere it appears.

PR

Shaped media and reputation work that builds credibility with health-conscious readers.

Digital Marketing

Planned content, social and search activity that reaches today's buyers where they look.

Healthcare and Wellness approach

Ayurveda and wellness brands win on authenticity. Communication has to be honest about what a product does, respectful of tradition and clear enough for a new buyer to trust in seconds.
