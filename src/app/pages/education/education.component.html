When should we start admissions marketing?

Ideally three to four months before forms open, with brand content running all year.

Can you photograph children?

Only with written parental consent and the school's approval, following your safeguarding policy.

Do you handle open day events?

Yes. From invitations and campaigns to on-ground branding and follow-ups.

Can you track which campaigns bring admissions?

Yes. We set up enquiry tracking so every admission can be traced back to its source.
