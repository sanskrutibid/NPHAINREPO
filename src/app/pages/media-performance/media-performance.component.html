The right message, to the right people, at the right time.

Good media is about placement as much as budget. We match each message to the audience, channel and moment where it will do the most work.

  • Audience-first planning: we start with who your buyers are, not which platform is trending.
  • Channel mix built across Meta, Google, YouTube, WhatsApp and local outdoor.
  • Creative tested against each audience before budgets scale.
  • Every rupee tracked to enquiries, site visits or sales.

Questions we often hear.

What budget do we need?

It depends on your market and goal. We will suggest a starting budget in the first conversation and scale only when results justify it.

Do you charge a percentage of spend?

We offer both fixed management fees and spend-based models. We will recommend the one that suits your scale.

Will we own our ad accounts?

Always. Accounts, pixels and data stay in your name.

How soon will we see results?

Most campaigns show clear signals in two to four weeks. Real optimisation comes from the first two to three months of data.
