About the client

Samira Habitats is a real estate developer known for plotted land around Alibaug on the Konkan coast. Many buyers of plots on this coast live in nearby cities, so they often decide before they have seen the land.

The brief

Samira Habitats worked with Phian on a plotted development on the Konkan coast. The brief was to give the project a steady social presence, to show the land through video, and to give the sales team a deck for investors and buyers.

What we did

Content

Planned and produced social content for the project.

AI video

Produced AI-assisted walkthrough and explainer videos, so buyers can see the project without visiting first.

Sales

Created the investor and buyer pitch deck.

Media

Supported the launch with targeted campaigns.
