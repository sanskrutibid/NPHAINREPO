Brand architecture

Naming

Names for companies, projects and products, checked for meaning, availability and domains.

Is this right for you?

You will get the most from brand strategy and identity if any of these sound familiar.

  • You are launching a new project, product or business and want it to start strong.
  • Your brand looks different on every channel and nobody owns the whole picture.
  • You have grown into several businesses and the group story no longer makes sense.
  • Competitors with weaker products are winning because they tell a clearer story.

How it runs.

  1. Discover

    Workshops with your leadership and a review of everything you have today.

  2. Research

    Market, competitor and buyer research, summarised in a short findings deck.

  3. Define

    Positioning, messaging and architecture, agreed with you before any design.

  4. Design

    Two or three identity routes, refined into one system.

  5. Roll out

    Brand book, templates and a launch plan across every touchpoint.

Questions we often hear.

How long does a rebrand take?

Most identity projects take four to ten weeks, depending on research depth and how many brands are involved. A group brand architecture project can take longer.

Do we have to change our logo?

Not always. Sometimes the strategy shows the logo is fine and the problem is how the brand is used. We will tell you honestly.

Can you work with our existing agency?

Yes. We can deliver the strategy and brand book and hand over to your current teams, or run the other pillars ourselves.

What does it cost?

Every project is scoped after a first conversation. You get a fixed quote with deliverables listed before any work starts.
