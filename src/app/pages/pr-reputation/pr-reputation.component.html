Questions we often hear.

Can you guarantee coverage?

No one can guarantee editorial coverage. We can promise strong angles, persistent pitching and honest reporting of what landed.

Do you handle founder LinkedIn?

Yes. We plan, draft and edit founder posts in the founder's own voice, approved before publishing.

Can you remove negative reviews?

We cannot remove genuine reviews, and would not try. We help you respond well and build a stronger record over time.

Do you manage events?

Yes. From site launches to brand experiences, planned and run with your team.
